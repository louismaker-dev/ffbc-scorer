FFBC Page Locker

Encrypts a page with a club password, so only members who know it can open it. This all happens on this computer; nothing is uploaded.

Then
  1. Upload the downloaded index.html to your GitHub repo (replace the old one).
  2. Share the link and the password with members.
  3. To change the password, lock the original page again with a new one and re-upload. Everyone then needs the new password.

Use at least 8 characters; a few words together (like smash-rally-2026) is strong and easy to remember. Keep the unlocked original safe on your PC.
